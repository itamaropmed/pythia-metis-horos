# Pythia research plan after the declared-modulus audit

## Objective

Develop the strongest valid, training-free attainable-risk certificate that
can be automatically composed across tabular and language representations,
while refusing claims that the available data cannot identify.

## Priority 1 — Finish the discrepancy resource ladder

Status: partially completed.

1. Declared aggregate discrepancy curve: implemented.
2. Declared feature-modulus sufficient condition: implemented.
3. Exact repeated-environment calibration: implemented and confirmed.
4. Hard per-unit support geometry: implemented and confirmed.
5. Soft-tail or bounded-moment replacement for unavailable hard supports:
   open.
6. Clustered/temporal repeated environments: open.

Acceptance: every route must name the resource that identifies its penalty,
carry provenance, account for selection, and refuse ordinary one-draw rows
when the resource is absent.

## Priority 2 — Replace first-order action drift by certified curvature

Known-law diagnostics identify the transfer penalty—not concentration—as the
dominant remaining loss for MAE and pinball.  A second-order Jensen-gap route
is implemented for declared action variance and expected-loss curvature.  It
also optimizes the quadratic centre using unit-specific curvature rather than
defaulting to an arithmetic centre.

The identification ladder is now:

1. declared action variance/curvature sensitivity profile;
2. exact repeated-environment quantile intervals plus interval-box dispersion;
3. local-curvature domains guarded by simultaneous maximum action deviation;
4. refusal for one-outcome-per-unit data without an external declaration.

Acceptance: the learning-half-cap confirmation must show zero known-floor
exceedances and mechanism-level improvement over the first-order barycentric
route.  A separate untouched battery must then test the non-oracle repeated-
environment dispersion calibration.  The classical Jensen/smoothness lemma
is not a novelty claim; novelty is assessed for the composed finite-sample
certificate and its resource/refusal contract.

## Priority 3 — Repair extreme-quantile concentration

Q90 remains the weakest declared-modulus loss.  Develop a multi-point
pinball witness whose optimism and barycentric transfer penalty share one
frozen alpha ledger.  Compare against paired Bentkus, finite-policy Bernstein,
and the existing single-point/block witnesses on new mechanisms with skew,
rare tails, mixtures, and note-like concepts.

Acceptance: zero known-floor exceedances, multiplicity-controlled superiority
at the mechanism level, and visible negative results.  No universal-best
claim is allowed from a selected synthetic registry.

Progress: capped entity-McDiarmid overlap was valid but lost to disjoint
dKelly.  An arithmetic mixture of overlapping partition dKelly e-values was
also valid and slightly tighter on fresh exact-law data, but achieved only
8/10 mechanism-median wins against matched disjoint dKelly (`p=0.0547`).  The
next test was a frozen geometry-weighted mixture under nonzero, partition-
specific barycentric penalties.  Development completed and the best soft
weighting lost to the single minimum-penalty partition on 9/10 mechanism
medians.  No confirmation was warranted.  Disjoint dKelly remains the
default; further partition-mixture optimization is deprioritized.

## Priority 4 — Dependence and shift

Extend score and penalty events from iid environments to declared clusters or
time blocks.  Separate three assumptions:

- dependence among units inside an environment;
- dependence between environment rows;
- transfer from calibration environments to the target period.

Acceptance: theorem-level sampling-unit statement, latent-shock simulations,
and refusal when cluster/time provenance is missing.

## Priority 5 — Representation-gap edges for text

Finite decision/policy gap edges work for small registered concept alphabets,
not arbitrary raw text.  The next feasible routes are randomized acquisition,
prospective masking, or an external information/discrepancy cap.  Character
shuffle and failed learners remain falsification diagnostics, never proof of
sufficiency.

Acceptance: target-disclosure tests collapse the certified floor as expected;
irrelevant perturbations do not receive an assumed zero gap; raw text refuses
without a bridge.

## Priority 6 — External comparator fidelity

Implement author-faithful comparator protocols where artifacts exist,
starting with Anderson--Bjarnadottir.  Distinguish point floor estimators from
confidence lower endpoints and match data resources.  Direct SAA must be shown
whenever repeated target environments are available.

## Mayo gate

Mayo is a retrospective application, not a source of theorem selection.
Before numerical transfer:

1. reproduce and hash the exact cohort and honest OOF incumbent predictions;
2. freeze endpoint, representation, patient/time sampling unit, and note
   provenance;
3. identify a valid representation-gap or discrepancy resource;
4. otherwise publish only sensitivity curves and structured refusals.

The current one-outcome-per-case Mayo cohort cannot use repeated-environment
calibration, and it has no justified unit-level hard residual supports.
