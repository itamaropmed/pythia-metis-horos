# Frozen repeated-modulus V1 router

Development seeds `133001--133100` showed that diversified Kelly improved the
absolute-cost upper endpoint while Bentkus remained stronger for the squared
cost.  Before opening seeds `134001--134100`, V1 was frozen as:

- absolute barycentric discrepancy: dKelly upper endpoint;
- squared barycentric discrepancy: Bentkus upper endpoint;
- family alpha `.05`, split equally between the two endpoints;
- otherwise the identical ten mechanisms, 80 units, blocks, metric, clip, and
  500-environment design from the initial registration.

Primary endpoints are validity against both analytic population costs.
Secondary endpoints compare cap/truth ratios with the prior three-method
portfolio.  MSE is expected to remain range-limited; the router is not claimed
to solve that gap.
