# Registered finite-refinement signal confirmation

Written before opening seeds `65001`--`65050`.

## Frozen design

- The ten bounded residual mechanisms used by the representation batteries.
- 50 fresh seeds per mechanism and 1,024 entities per seed.
- Coarse representation: 20-level procedure.
- Informative refinement: a binary pre-outcome concept that shifts the
  conditional outcome law.
- Null refinement: an independent four-level nuisance label.
- Statistic: empirical MAE SAA at procedure minus empirical MAE SAA at
  `(procedure, refinement)`.
- 99 within-procedure permutations and plus-one Monte Carlo p-values.
- Two registered test families, assessed at 0.025 each.

## Primary endpoints

1. informative-concept rejection in at least 450/500 runs;
2. informative-concept rejection in at least 80% of runs for every mechanism;
3. no more than 40/500 nuisance-null rejections;
4. no model training and no outcome-derived refinement labels.

The test concerns conditional signal, not the magnitude of population
Bayes-risk reduction.  Mechanisms are the scientific comparison unit.
