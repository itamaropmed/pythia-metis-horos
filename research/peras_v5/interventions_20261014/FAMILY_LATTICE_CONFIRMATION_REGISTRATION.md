# Registered family/neighborhood lattice confirmation

Written before opening seeds `63001`--`63050`.

## Frozen data-generating mechanisms

Use the same ten bounded residual families as the earlier intervention
battery.  Each row has a 20-level procedure, its five-level procedure family,
and a binary pre-outcome note concept.  The concept shifts the conditional
outcome law in opposite directions.  An independent 16-level random token is
then appended to the richest representation.

The registered information graph is

```
(procedure, concept, nuisance) -> (procedure, concept)
                               -> procedure -> family -> global
```

The first arrow is additionally registered as an equal-risk relation because
the nuisance token is generated independently of the outcome conditional on
procedure and concept.  All conditional hard ranges are analytic from the
known finite support grids.  The lattice uses equal Bonferroni allocation at
family alpha 0.05 and performs no model training.

## Confirmation size

- 10 mechanisms;
- 50 untouched seeds per mechanism;
- 2,048 iid entities per seed;
- 500 simultaneous five-node lattice certificates (2,500 node endpoints).

## Primary endpoints

1. zero node lower endpoints above their known Bayes risks;
2. zero order violations after lattice closure;
3. independent-nuisance raw tightness lower than its canonical neighborhood
   counterpart in a majority of runs;
4. equality closure restores the nuisance node to the canonical neighborhood
   lower endpoint in every completed run;
5. positive canonical neighborhood bounds in every mechanism;
6. no model training and exact family alpha spend 0.05.

Mechanisms, not repeated seeds, are the unit for any scientific sign test.
The experiment evaluates the registered theorem and its practical power; it
does not establish the independence provenance that a real clinical nuisance
channel would require.
